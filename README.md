# Business Analytics & Decision Modelling

## 📋 Overview

A data analytics and decision-modelling project using R to solve real-world business problems across **financial investment, production planning, electric vehicle markets, and cryptocurrency data**.

The project combines financial modelling, optimisation, statistical analysis, data visualisation, and time-series analysis to demonstrate how data can be used to support business decisions.

## 🎯 Project Purpose

The project explores how analytical techniques can be applied to:

* Evaluate the financial viability of business investments.
* Optimise production decisions under limited resources.
* Analyse trends within the electric vehicle market.
* Investigate cryptocurrency price movements and volatility.
* Translate quantitative analysis into practical business insights.

## 📊 Analysis & Modelling

### 1. Investment & Financial Analysis

Developed an R-based financial model to evaluate an electric vehicle investment using **Net Present Value (NPV)**.

The analysis included:

* Discounted cash flow calculations.
* Present value analysis.
* A reusable R function for testing different discount rates.
* Sensitivity analysis to identify the approximate break-even discount rate.

### 2. Production Optimisation

Applied **linear programming** to determine the most profitable production mix while considering constraints such as assembly capacity, semiconductor availability, production time, and contractual requirements.

The analysis compared multiple scenarios to examine how changes in resource availability affected production decisions and profitability.

### 3. Electric Vehicle Market Analysis

Explored a real-world electric vehicle dataset containing information on:

* Battery capacity
* Driving range
* Price
* Top speed
* Charging speed
* Efficiency
* Acceleration

Created visualisations to identify relationships between vehicle specifications and answer key market questions.

### 4. Cryptocurrency Time-Series Analysis

Analysed a large cryptocurrency trading dataset to investigate price movements and volatility.

The analysis involved:

* Data transformation and cleaning.
* Handling missing observations.
* Time-series analysis.
* Daily log-return calculations.
* Price and volatility visualisations.
* Comparing market behaviour across different cryptocurrencies and trading pairs.

## 💡 Key Insights

The project demonstrated several important business and analytical findings:

* Investment profitability changes significantly depending on the discount rate.
* Production optimisation can reveal the most profitable product mix under resource constraints.
* Increasing the availability of one resource can expose another resource as the new production bottleneck.
* EV specifications such as battery capacity, range, price, and efficiency show measurable relationships that can support market analysis.
* Cryptocurrency returns demonstrate periods of increased volatility associated with major market events.
